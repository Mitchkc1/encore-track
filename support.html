Common questions

My show isn't on setlist.fm.
Choose "Enter a show manually" in Add a Show. You can still add the setlist yourself, and Encore Track finds the artwork for you.

How do I get my shows onto a new iPhone?
Sign in to the same iCloud account and they sync automatically (check Settings → iCloud in the app). You can also use Settings → Export backup, then Restore from backup on the new phone.

A friend sent me a show. How do I add it?
Tap the file they sent, then tap Encore Track (or Share → Encore Track). The app asks whether to add it.

Why can't I see my photos on my other devices?
Photos stay in your photo library. If iCloud Photos is on, they'll appear on your other devices too.

Is my data private?
Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
