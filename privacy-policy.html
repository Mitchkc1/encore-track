Privacy Policy

Encore Track · Last updated 26 November 2026

Encore Track is a personal concert diary. It's built so your information stays yours: there's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers that receive your data.

What we collect

Nothing. We (the developer) don't collect, store, sell or have access to any of your information.

Where your shows are kept

  • On your iPhone. The shows you log, ratings, notes, who you went with, seats and prices are stored on your device.
  • In your own iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, Encore Track syncs your shows to your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see it.
  • Backups you export. If you export a backup or spreadsheet, it goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

Services the app talks to

To look up shows, Encore Track sends what you search for (such as an artist, venue, city or date) to:

  • setlist.fm, for concerts and setlists (setlist.fm privacy policy).
  • Apple's iTunes Search API, for artist names, album artwork and 30-second song previews (Apple privacy policy).
  • MusicBrainz and Wikimedia (Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons), for artist details and bios (using the artist's name) and venue photos (using the venue's name, city and map location). A venue photo you pick, including one from your own library, stays on your iPhone (Wikimedia privacy policy, MetaBrainz privacy policy).
  • Apple Maps, for venue maps and Look Around photos (using the venue's map location).

These requests don't include your name, contact details or anything from your shows beyond the search itself.

Saving playlists. Only when you tap “Save as playlist” and choose Apple Music, Encore Track asks Apple Music for permission, looks up those songs and creates the playlist in your own Apple Music library. The song list goes to Apple; nothing comes to us.

Permissions

Encore Track asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them, and you can turn any of them off in the Settings app:

  • Photos: to show and suggest photos you took at a concert. Photos stay in your photo library; Encore Track only remembers which ones belong to which show.
  • Calendar: to find past concerts in your calendar. Read on your iPhone only.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to turn what you say into shows. On supported iPhones this happens on the device; otherwise Apple's speech service processes it.
  • Notifications: to remind you about upcoming shows (the evening before, the day of, and the morning after). Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Apple Music: only when you save a playlist, to add it to your Apple Music library.
  • Location: only when you tap the location button, to find the city you're in. Your location isn't stored or sent anywhere except to look up the city name with Apple Maps.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your next show and this year's count. They read a small summary that Encore Track keeps on your iPhone.

Sharing a show

When you send a show to a friend, Encore Track creates a small file with the artist, venue, date, setlist and artwork. Your rating, notes, seat, price, photos and who you went with are never included. It's sent through the normal iPhone share sheet, to whoever you choose.

Together (shows shared with a partner)

If you invite someone to Together, the shows you put in Together (artist, venue, date, setlist, rating, notes and who you went with) are shared with them through Apple's iCloud sharing, and either of you can edit them. Photos are never shared. Your other shows stay private. You can remove shows or stop sharing at any time in Settings → Together. We can't see any of it.

Friends

If you choose to share your card, Encore Track saves a small summary to your own iCloud: the name you enter, your totals (shows, artists, venues, cities, countries, songs), your badges, your top artists, your latest show and your music taste by genre. Star ratings are only included if you switch them on. Notes, photos, seats, prices and who you went with are never included. Anyone you send your link to who opens it in Encore Track can see your card, read-only, through Apple's iCloud sharing. You can stop sharing at any time, and your link then stops working. We don't run a server for this and can't see your card or your friends'.

Shared albums

If you start a shared album for a show, Encore Track saves it in your own iCloud with the show's artist, venue, city and date, and shares it by link through Apple's iCloud sharing. Anyone you send the link to who opens it in Encore Track can see the album and add photos. Photos you add are shrunk and uploaded with the name you set in Friends. You can remove your own photos at any time; whoever started the album can delete it for everyone, and anyone else can leave it. Only the photos you choose to add are shared. We can't see any of it.

Children

Encore Track doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete any show in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Encore Track → Delete Data. To remove your Friends card, tap Stop Sharing Your Card in Friends → Share Your Card.
